INFORMATION HANDLING

Privacy Policy

CRIT-SPARE processes the shop name, operator name, station, email address, and phone number used for verification when a shop account is created. Operators may also add inventory, quantity, bin, audit, invoice, and activity records.

How information is used

This information supports sign-in, phone verification, shop setup, inventory management, billing, audit history, and service protection. Shop records are associated with the shop and operator account that created them.

Service providers

Firebase Authentication handles account sign-in and Cloud Firestore stores shop data. Firebase phone verification and Google reCAPTCHA process phone verification data to help prevent abuse. Google Fonts receives ordinary browser requests for the typefaces and interface icons displayed by the site.

Storage and choices

The site uses browser storage for sign-in state and a remembered sign-in preference. reCAPTCHA may use security-related cookies. The current site does not use advertising or analytics tracking. For questions about account or shop data, contact your shop manager or project administrator.

Read the full Privacy Policy

BROWSER STORAGE

Cookies & Storage

CRIT-SPARE currently uses storage needed for sign-in, phone verification, and your saved sign-in preference. It does not currently use advertising or analytics cookies.

Authentication storage

Firebase keeps the signed-in state in browser storage. “Remember sign-in on this device” keeps a session across browser restarts; with it off, the session lasts only for the current browser session.

Security cookies

Google reCAPTCHA may use cookies or similar storage to protect phone verification from abuse. Phone verification requires this security check.

Preference storage

A small browser entry remembers your sign-in-storage choice. You can change that choice using “Manage cookies & sign-in storage” on the sign-in page. Clearing site data in your browser signs you out and removes saved preferences.
