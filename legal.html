01 • USING THE SERVICE

Terms and Conditions

These terms apply when you use CRIT-SPARE to manage a shop’s spare-parts inventory, operator records, audits, and bills. Creating an account means you agree to these terms.

Accounts and access

Provide accurate account and shop details. Keep your password and verification codes private. You are responsible for activity performed with your account and for deciding which operators should have access to your shop.

Inventory and business records

Enter and review stock movements, prices, invoices, and audit counts carefully. The service helps organize inventory records; it does not replace your own accounting records, tax filings, or professional advice. Confirm business-critical information before relying on it.

Acceptable use and service changes

Do not attempt to access another shop’s data, interfere with the service, bypass security controls, or use the service unlawfully. Access may be limited where needed to protect accounts, data, or service operation. Features may change as the service is maintained.

Questions

For questions about an account or shop, contact the person who manages that shop.

02 • INFORMATION HANDLING

Privacy Policy

This policy describes information processed by the current CRIT-SPARE website.

Information you provide

When creating a shop, the service processes the shop name, operator name, chosen station, email address, password credential, and phone number used for verification. Operators may also create inventory, quantity, bin, audit, invoice, and activity records.

How the information is used

The information supports account access, phone verification, shop setup, inventory management, billing, audit history, and protection of the service. Shop records are associated with the shop and operator account that created them.

Service providers

Firebase Authentication handles account sign-in and Firebase Cloud Firestore stores shop data for this project. For phone verification, Firebase sends and stores phone numbers with Google to help prevent abuse across Google services. Google reCAPTCHA checks that verification requests are not automated. The page also loads typefaces and interface icons from Google Fonts, which receives ordinary browser requests for those files.

Browser storage and tracking

The site uses browser storage for Firebase sign-in state and a remembered sign-in preference. Google reCAPTCHA may use security-related cookies. The current site does not use advertising or analytics tracking.

Managing information

Use the sign-out control on a shared device and choose whether to remember sign-in on your device. For questions or a request about account or shop information, contact your shop manager or the project administrator. The operator responsible for this deployment should provide a direct support contact before public launch.

03 • COOKIES & BROWSER STORAGE

Cookies and Storage

CRIT-SPARE uses only storage needed for the features described below. The app does not currently set advertising or analytics cookies.

Authentication storageFirebase keeps the signed-in state in browser storage. Choose “Remember sign-in on this device” to keep the session across browser restarts; leave it off to keep the session to the current browser session.
Security cookiesGoogle reCAPTCHA may use cookies or similar storage to protect phone verification from abuse. Phone verification is unavailable without this security check.
Preference storageA small local browser entry remembers your sign-in-storage choice so it can be applied consistently.

You can change the remembered sign-in choice from the cookie and sign-in storage controls on the sign-in page. You can also clear site data in your browser; clearing it signs you out and removes saved site preferences.

This page reflects the current features in this deployment. The project owner should keep these details current when the service, storage, or providers change.
