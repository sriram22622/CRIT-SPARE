Privacy Policy

Plain-language information about how CRIT-SPARE processes shop inventory, operator profiles, and authentication records.

Effective Date: 17 November 2026

01 • INFORMATION WE COLLECT

What information is processed

When you create and manage a shop account, we process the necessary information to keep your station operational:

  • Account Details: Operator name, email address, password credential, and station identifier.
  • Phone Verification: Mobile phone number for SMS OTP security checks to prevent automated abuse.
  • Shop Records: Shop name, inventory parts, quantities, bin locations, invoices, and audit counts created by your operators.

02 • HOW WE USE INFORMATION

Purpose of processing

We process information solely to provide inventory management, billing, operator authentication, and fraud prevention. We do not sell operator data, nor do we use advertising trackers or third-party behavioral profiling.

03 • INFRASTRUCTURE & SECURITY

Service providers and security

Our application uses industry-standard Cloud infrastructure:

  • Firebase Authentication: Manages secure passkey and token authentication sessions.
  • Cloud Firestore: Stores shop inventory, invoices, and activity logs protected by granular per-shop security rules.
  • Google reCAPTCHA: Defends phone verification against bot spam and automated attacks.
  • Encrypted Transport: All data in transit is encrypted using modern TLS (HTTPS) with Strict-Transport-Security.

04 • YOUR CHOICES & STORAGE

Browser storage & account controls

You can manage session persistence using the "Remember sign-in on this device" preference on the sign-in page. You may update your profile details at any time from your dashboard or contact your shop administrator to request complete record deletion.
